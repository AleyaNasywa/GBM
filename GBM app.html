The Excel Discretization Formula

ΔS = St × (μΔt + σε√(Δt))

Next Price St+1 = St + ΔS

Every step in stock prices has two simple components: a predictable growth trend (drift) plus an unpredictable market bounce (volatility shock).

St

Current Price Scale

Stock movements are percentage-based. A 2% gain on a $100 stock is $2; on a $1,000 stock it is $20.

μΔt

Drift (Expected Growth)

The expected annual return (μ) scaled down to the period size (Δt). The steady upward or downward pull.

σε√(Δt)

Market Shock (Volatility)

Uncertainty swing. Volatility scales with the square root of time because market risk compounds over time.

ε ~ N(0,1)

Random Dice Roll

Standard normal distribution shock. In Excel, this is generated using NORM.S.INV(RAND()).

What Is This Used For?

Instead of guessing a single future, Monte Carlo simulates thousands of possibilities so decision makers can manage real-world risk.

🏖️

Retirement Planning

Checks if your pension savings will survive bad market years. If 95 out of 100 simulations keep you funded until age 95, your retirement plan is safe.

🏦

Bank Stress Testing

Regulators require banks to simulate severe market crashes to prove they hold enough backup cash reserves to prevent insolvency.

📜

Stock Option Pricing

Calculates fair prices for exotic contracts where payout depends on the highest or lowest price reached during the entire year.

🏭

Corporate Investments

Companies model uncertain factory costs and fluctuating commodity prices before deciding whether to approve multi-million dollar projects.
